## Output

Write or update [writeup/draft.md](../../../writeup/draft.md):

- Goal and data (what is known vs unverified)
- What was tried
- What failed and **why** (from `failures.md`, not a smoother story)
- Current method
- Related work (from `related-work.md` only, unless the user asked to add a citation)
- What is **not** claimed (no µm/s or tracking accuracy unless lab notes support it)

Do not invent frame counts, speeds, or results. If the notebook does not say it, say it is unknown.
